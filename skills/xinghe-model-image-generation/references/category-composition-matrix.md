# Category Composition Matrix

Read this file before writing the 8-image plan. Use it to adapt the default set to the product category. Always keep the two-stage identity workflow: image 1 creates the model reference, images 2-8 reuse that model.

## General Rule

Do not force every product into an apparel-style sequence. Match the roles to how the product is actually worn, held, carried, applied, opened, adjusted, or presented on a pure white background.

If the user specifies roles, quantity, crop, or platform, the user request overrides this matrix.

## Apparel

Use for dresses, tops, pants, coats, underwear that is safe and non-sexualized, sportswear, and similar garments.

1. Full-body model identity reference.
2. Front half-body or three-quarter hero.
3. Full-body scale and fit.
4. Side angle.
5. Back or side-back angle.
6. Fabric/detail close-up with body contact.
7. Dynamic movement showing drape, stretch, or silhouette.
8. Premium campaign hero.

## Shoes

1. Full-body model identity reference with shoes worn.
2. Standing side profile.
3. Walking-step full-body.
4. Low-angle foot/shoe detail.
5. Sole/upper/heel structure close-up.
6. Seated or leaning pose showing fit.
7. Dynamic step with realistic foot contact.
8. Premium hero with shoes clearly visible.

## Bags

1. Full-body model identity reference carrying the bag.
2. Handheld front/three-quarter.
3. Shoulder/crossbody scale shot.
4. Side or back carrying angle.
5. Strap/hardware/closure detail.
6. Natural open/adjust/carry interaction.
7. Walking or turning pose with bag motion.
8. Premium hero with bag as main focus.

## Handheld Goods, Drinkware, Bottles, Cups

Use for thermos cups, tumblers, water bottles, mugs, umbrellas, small tools, phone cases, and similar hand-presented products.

1. Full-body model identity reference holding the product.
2. Half-body hand-held hero.
3. Full-body or wider scale proof.
4. Side or over-shoulder hand-held angle.
5. Product detail close-up: logo, lid, button, opening, texture, or finish.
6. Two-hand presentation or natural hold.
7. Dynamic offer/hand-off/gesture pose.
8. Premium campaign hero with product near face or torso.

Avoid making too many full-body poses for handheld products. Prioritize product visibility, hand scale, grip logic, detail clarity, and face-adjacent commercial appeal.

## Beauty and Skincare

1. Full-body or half-body model identity reference holding product.
2. Face-adjacent hero.
3. Hand-held product scale shot.
4. Cap/pump/applicator/detail close-up.
5. Application gesture without unsupported efficacy claims.
6. Texture/packaging-facing close-up when appropriate.
7. Clean dynamic hand movement.
8. Premium beauty hero.

Do not imply medical effects, before/after results, certification, or ingredient claims unless provided.

## Jewelry, Watches, and Small Accessories

1. Wider model identity reference wearing or holding the item.
2. Face/neck/ear/wrist/hand hero depending on product.
3. Body-scale proof.
4. Side angle showing depth or fastening.
5. Macro detail of material, stone, clasp, dial, or texture.
6. Natural adjustment gesture.
7. Refined pose with subtle motion.
8. Premium hero.

Preserve normal body-part scale. Do not enlarge small accessories into props.

## Small Electronics and Home Goods

1. Full-body or half-body model identity reference presenting the product.
2. Half-body product hero.
3. Hand-held scale shot.
4. Side/port/button orientation angle.
5. Detail close-up of controls, screen, port, texture, or opening.
6. Natural operation gesture without unsupported performance claims.
7. Dynamic hand-off or presentation pose.
8. Premium hero.

Avoid fake UI, fake certification, fake battery life, fake wattage, or invented specs.

## Ambiguous Products

If the category is unclear:

- choose the closest physical interaction mode: worn, carried, hand-held, face-adjacent, body-part close-up, or presented;
- include one full-body identity reference, one scale proof, two product detail images, two interaction images, one alternate angle, and one premium hero;
- do not ask the user unless the ambiguity would create a risky or inappropriate image.
