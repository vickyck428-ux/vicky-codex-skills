# Walmart US listing content policy

Use this policy for Walmart.com listings. This snapshot was verified on 2026-07-18 against Walmart's official US guidance:

- [Product details policy](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Item%20setup/Item%20content%2C%20imagery%2C%20and%20media/Product-Detail-Page%3A-overview), updated 2026-05-21.
- [Keyword optimization](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Item%20setup/Item%20content,%20imagery,%20and%20media/Product-detail-page:-Keyword-optimization?locale=en-US), updated 2026-04-14.
- [Image guidelines and requirements](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Item%20setup/Item%20content%2C%20imagery%2C%20and%20media/Product-detail-page%3A-Image-guidelines-%26-requirements?locale=en-US), updated 2026-05-12.

Recheck these pages when Walmart publishes a newer policy or the user supplies a newer category template. Apply the newer or stricter official category rule when it conflicts with this general policy.

## Product title

- Write a brief, clear, descriptive English title of at most 150 characters. Count spaces and punctuation when validating.
- Follow any stricter category-template limit. For the current handbag template, target at most 90 characters and warn above 100.
- Use proper capitalization and lead with the configured brand. Prefer `Brand + Product Type + Key Attribute + Size or Count + Main Differentiator` when the evidence supports those elements.
- Include only relevant values such as size, pack count, capacity, or weight.
- Do not use all caps, repeated keywords, multiple brands, multiple unrelated product types, URLs, irrelevant availability text, or unsupported years.
- Do not use promotional or unverifiable claims such as `Free Shipping`, `Walmart Fulfilled`, `Hot Sale`, `Top Rated`, `Premium Quality`, `Best Selling`, `Clearance`, `Black Friday`, `Savings`, or `Low Price`.
- Do not name Walmart, Amazon, Target, or another retailer unless the name is a licensed part of the product or brand.
- Use English only, except for an official brand or item name.

## Description

- Write one English paragraph of at least 150 words. Apply a higher category minimum when required; do not impose a general maximum unless the current template defines one.
- Start with the exact title. Describe the actual product's features, benefits, materials, sizes, colors, quantities, use, and limitations using evidence-backed facts.
- Include the product name, brand, and relevant customer search terms naturally.
- Do not use bullets, numbered lists, HTML, emojis, external URLs, retailer names, competitor-exclusivity claims, promotional claims, irrelevant availability text, or excessive keyword repetition.
- Do not make authenticity claims unless Walmart has approved them or the applicable policy explicitly allows them.
- Keep the description consistent with the title, images, attributes, and delivered product.

## Key Features

- Provide three to 10 features, most important first. Use exactly five when the selected template and tool expect five.
- Keep every feature at most 80 characters including spaces.
- Use short, distinct phrases. Do not use URLs, HTML, emojis, numbered lists, special bullet characters, promotional claims, retailer names, irrelevant information, or repeated keywords.
- Keep every feature consistent with the title and actual product.

## Keywords

- Select one or two primary keywords relevant to how a customer would naturally search.
- Use them naturally and no more than once per title, description, or feature phrase. Do not add irrelevant or repetitive search terms.

## Images

- Use actual images of the exact product. Do not use stock photography, placeholders for a final deliverable, duplicated images, or images belonging to another color variant.
- Target 2200 by 2200 pixels, RGB, 8 bits per pixel, 1:1 square, and 5 MB or less. Treat 1500 by 1500 pixels as the minimum for zoom and record a warning below that threshold rather than hiding it.
- Use 100 by 100 pixels for swatch images where applicable. Do not enlarge a small low-resolution source merely to meet a pixel target.
- Use JPEG, JPG, PNG, or BMP. Do not deliver GIF or WebP; convert an eligible source to JPEG or PNG without changing the product.
- Use a seamless white `255,255,255` background for the primary image. Keep the product tightly cropped, in focus, and professionally lit.
- Recommend at least four distinct images per listing. Record a warning when fewer than four eligible images exist.
- Do not show watermarks, seller names, retailer logos, text overlays, promotional claims, non-English text, or accessories not included with the item.
- Allow AI-assisted background removal or image cleanup only when the result is truthful, accurate, rights-compliant, and not misleading. Preserve the actual product's shape, color, texture, proportions, logos, straps, chains, and included accessories.
- Use a public image URL ending in a supported image extension and communicating on port 80, 443, 8080, or 8443. Do not use an HTML page, inaccessible URL, query string, Dropbox URL, or unsupported port.

## Attributes and evidence

- Make every title, description, feature, image, and attribute match the delivered product, including size, color, quantity, material, ingredients, features, benefits, and limitations.
- Fill as many attributes as the evidence supports. Use the correct data type and unit for each field; never insert placeholders or fabricated values.
- Use only content and images for which the seller has the necessary rights. Review and substantiate every AI-generated claim before export.
- Treat noncompliance as blocking because Walmart may remove the content or offer and may suspend or terminate the account.
